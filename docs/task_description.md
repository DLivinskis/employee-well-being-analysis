Your assignment is to build a data product around the Kaggle open dataset on remote work and mental health: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/waqi786/remote-work-and-mental-health . 



The Brief 



Build a data product that helps users explore and understand what drives employee satisfaction with remote work and lets them make predictions for individual employees based on the factors you find meaningful. 



The form is up to you — a web app, an API, a notebook-driven report, an agentic interface, and a dashboard. We care about the thinking behind the choices, not the format itself. 



Your product should address two questions: 




What are the primary factors influencing remote work satisfaction among employees? 

Given a set of employee attributes, what satisfaction level would you predict? 

 



How We'd Like You to Work 



We expect you to use AI coding assistants (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, or similar) as a natural part of your workflow — not as a shortcut, but as a force multiplier. This is how we work, and we want to see how you work with these tools. 



One thing we'll pay attention to: where you exercised judgment. AI tools are good at generating code; they're less good at knowing what to build, how to structure it, and when something is good enough. Show us where those decisions were yours. 



If your submission includes an agentic component — e.g. a conversational interface, an automated analysis loop, a tool-using assistant that answers questions about the data — that's interesting to us, though not a requirement.
